# 0 · "Suggest for me"

Automations → **Create automation** → **Suggest for me**.
Devin looks at the org's recent sessions, connected integrations and repositories and proposes
automations with trigger + prompt pre-filled. For this repo expect suggestions along the lines of
"implement ADO work items assigned to Devin", "fix failing CI on payments-api-demo",
"weekly dependency bump". Pick the ADO one → it opens pre-filled in the editor → continue with
`01-ado-work-item-assigned.md` to tighten conditions and limits.

Talking point: this is the entry ramp — the team does not need to know what to automate; Devin
reads the last month of sessions and tells them where the repetition is.

Alternative entry: **Generate with Devin** starts a session that builds the automation with you
(useful when the trigger is a custom webhook like ADO Service Hooks).
